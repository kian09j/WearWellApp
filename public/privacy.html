WearWell privacy

Last updated 11 October 2026 · WearWell is made by Polska Picks

The short version: your wardrobe stays on your phone. WearWell has no accounts, no tracking and no ads, and Polska Picks never receives your data. The one exception is the optional photo scan: only when you use it, the photo you choose is sent to Anthropic’s Claude AI to recognise the clothes, and it is not stored by WearWell.

What the app stores, and where

The pieces you add, their photos, the wears and outfits you log, and your settings are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. None of it is sent to Polska Picks or to anyone else.

Photos

When you take or choose a photo, it is resized on your device and saved there. Saving it this way also removes hidden details a camera adds, such as the location where the photo was taken. Photos of your pieces are never uploaded. (The optional photo scan, described below, is the only time a photo leaves your phone.)

Backups

"Export backup" creates a file that you decide where to save, for example in your Files app, cloud storage or email. Polska Picks never receives it. Importing a backup reads a file you choose.

Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone. No server is involved and nothing is sent.

Other websites

"Open Vinted" and links to polskapicks.com open in your browser, where those websites' own privacy policies apply. WearWell does not send them anything: the listing text only goes to Vinted if you copy and paste it yourself.

Analytics, ads and cookies

WearWell uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The web version is served by a hosting provider which, like any website host, processes basic technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the app's files.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → "Erase everything" in the app, or uninstall the app, and everything it stored on your device is removed.

Wardrobe photo scan (optional)

The photo scan helps you add many pieces at once. It only runs when you choose to use it, and the app asks for your agreement before the first scan.

  • What is sent: the one photo you take or choose, made smaller on your phone first (which also removes location data), plus a random code created by the app. The code lets the server limit how many scans each phone can do per day; it is not linked to your name, email or anything else.
  • Where it goes: over an encrypted connection to WearWell’s server, which passes the photo to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI, to recognise the clothes in it. The list of pieces comes back to your phone, where you check it before anything is added.
  • What is kept: WearWell’s server does not store or log the photo. To enforce the daily limit it keeps a count of scans per random code and per IP address, which expires after about a day. Anthropic processes the photo under its own terms and privacy policy.

Changes

If WearWell ever changes what leaves your device, this page will be updated first, and the app will explain what is sent and ask before anything leaves your phone.
